## Full description

**English:**

> GLiPra is a GLP-1 nutrition companion built by a licensed pharmacist. If you are on Ozempic,
> Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound, Saxenda, Trulicity, or a compounded GLP-1, GLiPra helps you stay
> on top of the protein that supports muscle while your medication does its job.
>
> WHAT YOU CAN DO
> - Track your daily protein against a target calculated from your profile.
> - Log meals by photo, by voice, by barcode, or by searching a pharmacist-curated food database.
> - Follow your injection cycle and see which phase you are in between doses.
> - Log injection sites with a rotation reminder.
> - Watch key micronutrients that matter on a reduced appetite.
> - Note side effects and get a clear prompt to contact your prescriber when it matters.
> - Prepare for prescriber visits with a summary you can export.
>
> BUILT BY A PHARMACIST
> GLiPra is built by a licensed pharmacist and uses pharmacist-authored educational content.
> It is designed to help you stay informed between prescriber visits.
>
> FREE AND PRO
> Manual food logging, barcode scanning, food database search, side-effect escalation,
> micronutrient watch, data export, and account deletion are always free. GLiPra Pro adds AI
> photo recognition, voice logging, daily AI guidance, unlimited protein history, and prescriber
> visit prep with PDF export.
>
> IMPORTANT
> GLiPra is an educational and tracking application for general wellness purposes. Information
> provided, including AI-generated suggestions and content articles, is for educational purposes
> only and is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Although GLiPra was designed by a
> licensed pharmacist, your use of GLiPra does not establish a pharmacist-patient relationship
> or any professional medical relationship. GLiPra is not a substitute for professional medical
> advice. Always seek the advice of your prescriber or qualified healthcare provider. If you
> think you may have a medical emergency, call 911.

> - Registra comidas por foto, por voz, por código de barras o buscando en una base de alimentos curada por un farmacéutico.
