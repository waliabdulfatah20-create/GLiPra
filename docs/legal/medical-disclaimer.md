## 3. Medication Level Estimates

The medication level estimator is a mathematical tool based on published pharmacokinetic half-life data for GLP-1 receptor agonists. It provides **estimates only**.

**Actual serum drug concentrations vary significantly based on individual factors including:**
- Individual metabolic rate
- Body composition and body weight
- Kidney and liver function
- Injection technique and site
- Absorption variability
- Concurrent medications
- Timing of meals and other factors

The estimated values displayed in the app **do not reflect your actual serum drug level** and should never be used to make decisions about medication dosage, timing, or continuation. Only a licensed healthcare provider can order and interpret serum drug level testing.

**Symptom-escalation notices:**
- Are not a diagnosis.
- Are not a determination that you are experiencing a medical emergency.

- May be triggered in situations that do not require urgent medical attention, and may not be triggered in all situations that do.

**If you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency services) immediately. Do not wait for or rely on any in-app alert.**
