5. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Calculate your protein floor and daily nutrition targets
  • Track your injection cycle and estimate medication levels
  • Generate AI-assisted meal analyses when you use the photo feature
  • Send injection reminders and phase-based guidance
  • Display progress charts, streak history, and journey milestones
  • Identify symptom combinations that may warrant prescriber follow-up (symptom-escalation notices)
  • Generate prescriber visit prep summaries and PDFs on request
  • Improve the Service using aggregated, de-identified analytics
  • Comply with applicable law

AI features: When you use meal-photo or voice analysis, your photo or voice recording is transmitted to our servers and then to OpenAI (our third-party AI provider, located in the United States). We do not use your photos or recordings to train AI models, and OpenAI does not use them to train its models. Photos and recordings are deleted within 24 hours of analysis. AI prompts for coaching and guidance contain only anonymized context, never your name, email, or identifying information.

9. Washington Residents, My Health My Data Act

Washington State Rights

If you are a Washington state resident, the Washington My Health My Data Act (WMHMD Act) applies to your weight measurements, medication data, injection records, symptom reports, and meal data.

We use this consumer health data solely to provide the Service to you. We do not sell consumer health data.

Your rights under the WMHMD Act:

  • Access, confirm whether we collect your health data and obtain a copy
  • Deletion, request deletion of your health data
  • Withdraw consent, withdraw previously granted consent (does not affect prior lawful processing)
  • List of sharing, obtain a list of third parties with whom we shared your health data

To exercise these rights, email legal@glipra.com with subject "Washington Health Data Rights Request." We will respond within 45 days and may verify your identity before processing.

10. California Residents, CCPA / CPRA

California Rights

California residents have the right to: know what personal information we collect and how we use it; delete personal information; correct inaccurate information; opt out of sale or sharing (we do not sell or share for targeted advertising, no action needed); limit use of sensitive personal information (we use health data only to provide the Service); and non-discrimination for exercising these rights.

11. Texas Residents, TDPSA

Texas residents have the right to access, correct, delete, and obtain a portable copy of their personal data, and to opt out of the sale of personal data and targeted advertising. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.
