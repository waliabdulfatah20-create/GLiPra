The GLiPra difference

Your appetite is not constant. Your guidance should not be either.

Each injection follows a predictable cycle. GLiPra is built to adapt your targets to exactly where you are in it.

Day 0
Injection day
Nausea often peaks. Lean toward lighter protein.
Days 1-2
Peak suppression
Appetite at its lowest. Protein timing matters most.
Days 3-4
Adjustment
Appetite stabilizing. A good window to rebuild the habit.
Days 5-7
Recovery window
Appetite returning. Prioritize hitting your floor.
Day 8+
Overdue
Medication fading. Hunger climbs. GLiPra flags it.

On an oral GLP-1? GLiPra tracks your daily dose window and adherence instead, including the 30-minute empty-stomach timer.
