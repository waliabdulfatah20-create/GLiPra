## 6. How We Use Your Information

### 6.1 Core Service Delivery

- Create and manage your account
- Calculate your protein floor and daily nutrition targets
- Track your injection cycle and estimate medication levels
- Generate AI-assisted meal analyses when you use the photo feature
- Send you injection reminders and phase-based guidance
- Display your progress charts and streak history
- Identify symptoms that may warrant clinical escalation (symptom-escalation notices)

### 6.2 Safety Features

We use your symptom logs to generate **symptom-escalation notices** — automated alerts suggesting you contact your prescriber. These are rule-based triggers, not a clinical diagnosis. See Section 3 of the Terms of Service.

### 6.3 AI Features

When you use the meal-photo analysis feature, your meal photo is transmitted to our servers and processed using third-party AI services (see Section 10 — Subprocessors). We do not use your meal photos to train AI models. Photos are retained only as long as necessary to deliver the analysis result.

When you use the AI guidance or coaching features, we transmit **anonymized, non-identifying** context to our AI provider. We never include your name, email address, or other identifying information in AI prompts.

### 6.4 Product Improvement

We use **aggregated and de-identified** usage analytics to understand how features are used and where the app can be improved. This analytics data cannot be traced back to you as an individual.

### 6.5 Communications

- **Transactional:** Account confirmation, password reset, subscription receipts, and in-app notifications related to your logged data (injection reminders, symptom escalation, streak milestones).
- **Product updates:** If you opt in, we may send email or push notifications about new features or health guidance content. You may opt out at any time.

### 6.6 Security and Fraud Prevention

We use account data, IP addresses, and usage patterns to detect unauthorized access, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Service.

### 6.7 Legal Compliance

We process data as required to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful legal process, and protect our legal rights.

## 9. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures, including:

- **Encryption in transit:** All data transmitted between the app and our servers uses TLS 1.2 or higher.
- **Encryption at rest:** Data stored in our database is encrypted at rest by our database provider (Supabase).
- **Row-Level Security (RLS):** Our database enforces user-level data isolation — your data is inaccessible to other users at the database level.
- **Authentication:** Accounts are protected by email and password. We encourage strong, unique passwords.
- **Access controls:** Employee access to production data is restricted to personnel who need it to operate or support the Service.

No system is completely secure. In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in high risk to your rights, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

## 10. Consumer Health Data — Washington My Health My Data Act

If you are a **Washington state resident**, the Washington My Health My Data Act (WMHMD Act) provides you with specific rights regarding "consumer health data," which includes data that identifies your physical or mental health condition, medication use, or health-related behaviors.

### 10.1 Categories of Consumer Health Data We Collect

We collect the following consumer health data from Washington residents:
- Weight measurements
- Medication type, dosage, and injection dates
- Injection site logs
- Symptom reports
- Meal and nutrition data

### 10.2 How We Use and Share Consumer Health Data

We use consumer health data **solely to provide and improve the Service** to you. We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share consumer health data for advertising purposes or with data brokers.

Consumer health data is shared with the subprocessors listed in Section 7.1, all of which process data only to perform services for us under contractual restrictions.

### 10.3 Your Rights Under the WMHMD Act

Washington residents have the right to:
- **Access** — Confirm whether we collect your consumer health data and obtain a copy.
- **Deletion** — Request deletion of your consumer health data.
- **Withdraw consent** — Withdraw previously granted consent to our collection of consumer health data. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing.
- **List of third-party sharing** — Obtain a list of the specific third parties with whom we have shared your consumer health data (if any).

To exercise these rights, email [LEGAL@GLIPRA.COM] with the subject line "Washington Health Data Rights Request." We will respond within **45 days** and may verify your identity before processing the request.

### 10.4 Consent

By creating an account and using GLiPra, you provide the consent required under the WMHMD Act for our collection and use of consumer health data as described in this Policy. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting [LEGAL@GLIPRA.COM] or deleting your account.
