GLiPra-exclusive feature

Your appetite isn't constant.
Your guidance shouldn't be either.

Every GLP-1 injection follows a predictable 7-day cycle. GLiPra is the only app that adapts your protein targets and guidance to exactly where you are in that cycle.

Day 0
Injection Day
Nausea often peaks. Lighter protein sources recommended.
Days 1–2
Peak Suppression
Appetite at its lowest. Strategic protein timing matters most.
Days 3–4
Adjustment
Appetite stabilizing. Good window to rebuild protein habit.
Days 5–7
Recovery Window
Appetite returning. Prioritize hitting your protein floor.
Day 8+
Overdue
Medication wearing off. Hunger spikes. GLiPra flags this.
